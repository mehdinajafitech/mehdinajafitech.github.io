Aesthetic Hypothesis & Interactive Logic

Drawing upon formalist film theory, the engine operationalizes off-screen spatial tension (hors-champ) using generative video synthesis. When an acousmatic cue emerges beyond the visible frame, the system measures whether spectator gaze reorients toward the expectant negative space or remains fixated on the primary subject. If the peripheral void remains unattended beyond a calibrated temporal threshold, the finite state machine triggers an adaptive de-acousmatizing cut to confront the viewer with the off-screen threat, while a real-time Reinhard color transfer pipeline mitigates generative inter-shot visual drift.

System Architecture Pipeline

01. Capture: RGB Video Stream → 478-point Iris & Face Mesh
02. Pose & Gating: 3D solvePnP Stabilization & EAR Blink Filtering
03. Calibration: 2nd-degree Polynomial Ridge Regression
04. Perception: Asynchronous ROI Semantic Parsing on GenAI Rushes
05. Execution: Reactive Dual-Stream Switch & Reinhard LAB Color Harmonization
Methodology • Research-Creation

The Research Arc: From Directing to Adaptive Systems

PHASE I • ARTISTIC INQUIRY

Formalist Form & Space

Formulating aesthetic hypotheses on spatial tension, prolonged wide-angle framing, and acousmatic dread through short-film directing. These works serve as foundational ground truth for how compositional structure manipulates viewer psychology.

PHASE II • COMPUTATIONAL TURN

Gaze Sensing & Micro-Montage

Translating authored directorial intuition into quantitative parameters. Coupling generative video with real-time gaze telemetry to replace rigid linear playback with closed-loop, shot-level reactive montage engines.

PHASE III • DOCTORAL HORIZON

Adaptive Narrative & Simulation

Scaling implicit gaze and cognitive telemetry from micro-level shot transitions toward macro-level adaptive narrative engines. Investigating how viewer perceptual states dynamically orchestrate personalized, affect-driven story arcs—bridging the future of interactive streaming media and perceptual serious simulation testbeds.

System Role: Aesthetic Inquiry • Camera Motion & Shifting Negative Space

The Eternal Sleep

Written & Directed by Mahdi Najafi
▶
Watch Film on YouTube ↗
Directorial Foundation:

A linear short film exploring slow, controlled camera movement, wide-angle lenses, and shifting negative space. Rather than relying on locked-off shots, the camera moves deliberately through the environment, creating the persistent sensation of an unseen observer. The film examines how continuous camera motion alters composition and guides viewer focus across the frame, establishing the creative foundation for understanding how moving viewpoints influence spectator attention and suspense.

System Role: Aesthetic Inquiry • Off-Screen Space & Sound

KATZE

Written & Directed by Mahdi Najafi
▶
Watch Film on YouTube ↗
Directorial Foundation:

A linear short film examining off-screen tension (hors-champ) within a confined apartment setting. The film activates the space outside the frame through two core elements: the actor's gaze directed beyond the camera boundary and off-screen sound. This combination naturally draws the viewer's attention toward the edges of the screen. This directorial work established the conceptual premise for my adaptive system, demonstrating how staging and audio cues direct visual focus.

System Role: Aesthetic Inquiry • Low-Key Lighting & Visual Focus

Running Dark

Written & Directed by Mahdi Najafi
▶
Watch Film on YouTube ↗
Directorial Foundation:

A linear short film exploring visual attention and tension inside a car at night. Through low-key lighting, mirror reflections, and long takes, the film studies how spectator focus behaves in dark, low-contrast scenes where a threat can emerge at any moment. This directorial study helped shape my understanding of visual attention and pacing, investigating how lighting contrast and shot duration influence where viewers look.
