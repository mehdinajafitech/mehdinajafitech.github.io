Aesthetic Hypothesis & Interactive Logic

Drawing upon formalist film theory, the engine operationalizes off-screen spatial tension (hors-champ) using generative video rushes. When an acousmatic auditory cue emerges beyond the visible frame, the system measures whether spectator gaze reorients toward the expectant negative space or remains fixated on the primary subject. If the peripheral void remains unattended beyond a calibrated temporal threshold, the finite state machine triggers an adaptive de-acousmatizing cut to confront the viewer with the off-screen threat, while a real-time Reinhard color transfer pipeline mitigates generative inter-shot visual drift.

System Architecture Pipeline

01. Capture: RGB Video Stream → 478-point Iris & Face Mesh
02. Pose & Gating: 3D solvePnP Stabilization & EAR Blink Filtering
03. Calibration: 2nd-degree Polynomial Ridge Regression
04. Perception: Asynchronous ROI Semantic Parsing on GenAI Rushes
05. Execution: Reactive Dual-Stream Switch & Reinhard LAB Color Harmonization

The Research Arc: Research-Creation Trajectory

PHASE I • ARTISTIC INQUIRY

Directorial Form & Negative Space

Formulating aesthetic hypotheses on spatial tension, prolonged static wide-angle framing, and acousmatic dread through short-film directing. These artistic works serve as the foundational cinematic inquiry into how framing manipulates psychological dread without explicit visual exposure.

PHASE II • COMPUTATIONAL TURN

Generative Media & Adaptive Montage

Translating authored directorial intuition into quantitative parameters: pairing generative video synthesis with gaze telemetry to dynamically structure montage, replacing static timelines with reactive computational editing systems.

Directorial Study: Temporal Stasis & Gaze Restlessness

Pacing • Visual Saccade Dynamics

Deploys prolonged shot durations to provoke viewer visual restlessness. By withholding conventional cutting rates, this inquiry studies the point at which spectator attention wanders across the frame—a dynamic formalized in the dwell-time metrics of the system.
